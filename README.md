# AI-Job-Board-For-Teens

a safe job board for teens, powered by an AI job-finding agent A local-first jobs marketplace for 13–18 year olds. An AI agent crawls and normalizes real openings, employers manage AI-surfaced listings, neighbors post gigs like tutoring, lawn mowing and snow removal, and students filter, watch, and get notified the moment the right job appears.

Key Features:

Roles For students: one board of teen-appropriate jobs with strong filters, saved watches, and instant alerts. For employers: a review queue of AI-found candidate listings plus full self-serve job management. For the community: post paid or unpaid gigs (tutoring, lawn mowing, snow removal, pet care, babysitting) in minutes. For the platform: an autonomous AI agent that continuously discovers, normalizes, classifies teen-suitability, and de-duplicates real openings. Above all: safety, guardian visibility, and moderation are first-class, not bolted on.
